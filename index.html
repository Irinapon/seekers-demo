We are Seekers

We seek flavors, meaning and ideas. We love long breakfasts, fresh bread, good wine and the kind of conversation worth dropping everything for.

We bake our own bread, serve breakfast all day and believe that sometimes the best plan is not to rush.

    The Seekers’ rules of life

    While you’re here, be here

    01 / 06

    Rule01

    Put the laptop away

    We know work matters. But Seekers is a place for hedonism, easy conversation and loving glances.

    Enjoy the moment. And order more wine.
    Rule02

    Pastries — counter only

    Until our bakers are sure the croissants turned out just right, nothing leaves the oven for sale.

    Everything on the counter is ready for you.
    Rule03

    No table reservations

    We believe in everyone being equal, so seating is first come, first served. The room is small.

    Tables free up quickly.
    Rule04

    Order at the counter

    There are no waiters: our team will make your coffee, take your order and get your table ready.

    These croissants are worth the wait.
    Rule05

    Kids welcome

    High chair, kids’ cutlery, a changing table and busy boards.

    So you get to finish your croissant.
    Rule06

    Dogs welcome

    Pets up to 35 cm tall are welcome inside (there’s a height chart by the door); we ask bigger dogs to wait outside.

    And not on the windowsill.

    About us

    You don’t have to look far. Good things are simple: fresh bread, an unhurried breakfast, a glass of cider and a conversation you don’t want to end.

    That’s why people have breakfast here all day, watch the city go by, meet up, try new things and simply have a good time.
